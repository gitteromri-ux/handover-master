# AI Search Attribution

AI search attribution is the LLA measurement approach for identifying visits, leads, orders, and closed sales influenced by assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, and Gemini. It combines web referral detection with CRM persistence and self-reported discovery rather than treating traffic analytics as the complete record.[cite:1][cite:2]

## What it is

The implemented LLA path detects an AI source from referrer or campaign parameters, persists it for 30 days, attaches `ai_source` to CRM leads and orders through `DynamicParameters`, and emits an `ai_search_visit` data-layer event for analytics configuration.[cite:1][cite:2]

- **Measure through the CRM** — source capture follows the visitor into lead and order records so the organization can connect AI discovery to downstream sales, not only sessions.[cite:1][cite:2]
- **Use three complementary signals** — referral grouping in GA4 measures identifiable clicks, hidden form fields preserve the source in CRM, and “How did you hear about us?” captures recommendations followed by a manual brand search or direct visit.[cite:1]
- **Google AI Overviews are not a separate referrer** — their clicks arrive as ordinary Google organic traffic, so site-side referral logic cannot isolate them as a distinct source.[cite:1]
- **GTM remains the analytics activation layer** — the site pushes `ai_search_visit`, while GTM container `GTM-PMRKXXJV` still needs the matching custom-event trigger and GA4 event tag under an account with access.[cite:1]

# Chat Video Generation and Editing

Chat video generation and editing is a workflow split between what can run inside Perplexity and what requires external MCP-capable clients such as Claude or Cursor.[cite:1] For this user, the concept matters because video creation is used across French Atelier, Longevity Life Academy, ads, and board-facing decks.[cite:1][cite:2]

## What it is

Inside Perplexity, the available path is native video generation/editing plus file-based editing operations; external tools such as Higgsfield MCP, CapCut-style automation, and Reap-style MCP editing require clients that support custom MCP connectors.[cite:1]

- **Higgsfield runs outside Perplexity** — the hosted Higgsfield MCP URL can be added in Claude/Cursor-style connector panels, but Perplexity Skills are instruction documents and cannot install arbitrary MCP connectors.[cite:1]
- **Perplexity-native path stays in chat** — when the work must stay here, use native video generation models and chat-driven edits such as trimming, stitching, reframing, captions, audio overlays, speed, and export.[cite:1]
- **Skills are workflow codification, not tool access** — a Skill can preserve shot-list, prompt, aspect-ratio, and handoff rules, but it does not create a live Higgsfield or CapCut connection.[cite:1]
- **Professional editing standard** — after the user rejected Reap as insufficiently advanced, the recommended external stack became Higgsfield for generation plus Jumper for professional NLE editing inside Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, or Avid.[cite:1]
- **No-download connector preference** — the user later prioritized cloud/custom-connector workflows over downloadable editors; the discussed Claude-side stack was Higgsfield for generation and Reap for cloud editing, while Artgrid and Artlist remained manual asset libraries rather than personal-subscription MCP connectors.[cite:1]
- **Stock-bank connector split** — Freepik and Unsplash were discussed as connectable Claude-side stock/image sources through MCP or `npx` configuration, while Artgrid/Artlist were treated as download-and-feed asset sources.[cite:1]
- **Mockup videos use real source playback when possible** — the Longevity Life Academy ad-motion work accepted real screen recordings of animated banners inside phone frames, subtle UI sound design, and short 16:9/9:16 exports instead of AI-invented footage.[cite:3]
- **Fast SaaS-style animations can stay abstract** — when the brief is to animate mockups only, the accepted pattern is a short 1920×1080 sequence of phones landing, orbiting, and resolving to a hero phone, without headline/right-side explanatory text.[cite:4]
- **Prompt-only platform constraint** — when the user frames a builder as “AI only,” the workflow should be designed around prompt-driven generation and editing; InVideo AI was treated as the closest all-in-one prompt-first option, ElevenLabs Studio as strongest for prompt-described voice/audio, and precise timecode rhythm edits as the remaining weak spot that still needs region selection in current tools.[cite:5]
- **Large Drive video workaround** — when a large Drive source cannot be downloaded or uploaded reliably through the connector, the session’s workable path was to process the accessible source, attach the final file directly, and avoid claiming ElevenLabs availability when it is not actually connected.[cite:6]
- **Lyric micro-edits cannot be oversold as cloning-quality** — for a birthday reel, text removal via inpainting worked, but the DSP consonant morph from `live/living` to `Ziv/Ziving` did not convincingly read as “Ziv”; training or using a cloned model of an identifiable singer remained out of scope, so future offers should be kinetic typography/original-audio variants rather than promises of seamless vocal replacement.[cite:7]
