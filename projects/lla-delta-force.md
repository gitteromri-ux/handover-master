## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The hub organizes execution tracks for high-intent buying, lead vendors, influencers, and a large creative gallery rather than mixing activation planning into the production website.
- **Separate activation layer** — the hub keeps vendor, influencer, creative, and buying work in a dedicated support repository while the `Longevity-Academy` organization remains the production-site source of truth.
- **High-intent buying as a full track** — the buying tab is treated as a multi-section operating surface, not a single vendor list, while the other activation tracks remain independently navigable.
- **Remarketing and promo execution** — the activation hub includes recovery emails, remarketing banners, promo-email/landing-page work, and a large creative gallery aligned to the $279 monthly checkout offer.
- **Lead suppliers require exact-fit proof** — a vendor does not qualify as a high-intent source without documented live US longevity-course inventory, exclusive and consented leads, source transparency, replacement terms, qualification criteria, and comparable results. Generic education inquiries and agency/PPL models are not substitutes.
- **Julie automation brief** — the `#sandra` surface specifies eight funnels and 28 planned emails for the $79 live-masterclass campaign: new leads, three checkout-drop stages, two buyer-date flows, post-event Blueprint upsell, and no-show recovery.
- **Three-screen checkout map** — automation aligns to details, date/course selection, and payment-page stages. The earlier payment-method screen was removed because it does not exist.
- **No replay; one no-show transfer** — buyers who miss the first date move once to the remaining live date and re-enter its preparation/reminder flow. Post-event messaging uses a $200 Blueprint-course credit rather than a replay offer.
- **Brief hierarchy mirrors operating logic** — each funnel has its own color cluster, audience statement, and prominent email count so the email-marketing team can scan segments and touchpoints without implementation-role language.
