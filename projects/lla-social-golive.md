## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The pack combines post copy, platform-specific exports, officially sourced media, course and pricing context, enrollment calls to action, and published-article material for PR posts.
- **Source-to-post correlation** — each visual or clip must match the post's subject and come from an approved source; attractive but unrelated media is not acceptable.
- **Upload readiness is a QA gate** — sizing, cropping, sharpness, legibility, caption compliance, and public access must be checked before the pack is called final.
- **Real PR evidence** — PR posts use material from actual published articles rather than fabricated or mismatched outlet sheets.
- **Commercial completeness** — posts identify Longevity Life Academy and its course offer, include pricing where required, and direct readers to enroll through the LLA website.
- **Courtney sharing handoff** — `courtney-share.html` gives Courtney direct Instagram, Facebook, and website links, tagging instructions by post type, paste-ready school bio/caption copy, and a compact set of school facts and destination links.
