## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The dashboard models Longevity's July acquisition plan as a CEO-facing board surface for spend, contact rate, contacted-to-acquisition conversion, capacity, and target-risk decisions.
- **CEO-suite design reuse** — the dashboard was rebuilt from the CEO Performance Suite visual language after the user rejected earlier “premium” approximations; final commits added exact design-system styling, gradient chart treatments, datalabels, dark-glass tooltips, and fixed chart sizing.
- **Board-grade one-page compression** — the latest accepted shape is a single Executive Summary view with five KPIs, four board charts, four takeaways, and no tabs or annualized framing.
- **Target model as back-solved plan** — the board-grade build uses 25% contacted on leads and back-solves the close rate to 6.21% of contacted leads so the plan lands at 6 wins/week, $7,200 weekly revenue, 1.24× ROAS, and $967 CAC at a $1,200 acquisition price.
- **Interactive scenario layer** — a later version added four draggable inputs for weekly spend, CPL, contacted rate, and close rate, recalculating all views and allowing the $4,000/week, $16 CPL, 33% contacted, 4% close scenario to be compared instantly.
- **60-acquisition ramp variant** — the dashboard also gained a forward pilot path with contact rate rising 28%→46% and close rate rising about 3.8%→6.0%, landing on 60 acquisitions after roughly 9 weeks while exceeding the original $77,000 pilot cap by about $5,800.
- **Capacity treatment follows user framing** — earlier versions highlighted 3-rep overload against a 150-call ceiling, but the later user correction asked the model to show 40% contact and calls-per-lead math without presenting the 150-call ceiling as a constraint.
