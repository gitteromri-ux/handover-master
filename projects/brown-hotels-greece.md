## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The project has two linked surfaces: `play-paros-redesign` presents the proposed premium guest experience, while `brown-hotels-greece-briefing` explains the commercial and operational case behind it.
- **Transformation before decoration** — the proposition must connect F&B and service changes to a credible hotel experience and commercial opportunity; a visual refresh alone does not satisfy the brief.
- **Verified property truth** — hotel imagery and factual content must be real, property-relevant, and checked before publication. This avoids presenting generic or unrelated assets as PLAY Paros.
- **Concepts labeled as concepts** — proposed ideas such as the KATO F&B direction must be explicitly distinguished from the hotel's current facilities and services.
- **Public review surfaces** — both the redesign and executive briefing are delivered through public GitHub repositories and GitHub Pages rather than repo-only handoffs.
