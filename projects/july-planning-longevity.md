## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The surface translates French Atelier call-center performance data and Longevity rep/day data into July operating views: contacted-rate benchmarks, leads/shift behavior, calls-per-lead depth, per-rep actions, and weekly budget options.
- **French Atelier DB as benchmark source** — the user corrected the benchmark source to the French Atelier call-center performance DB rather than the CEO suite; the accepted framing used the leads/shift × contacted-rate intersect and avoided implying unavailable French per-rep or calls-field metrics.
- **Contacted rate definition** — contacted rate is contacted ÷ open-order leads, not contacted ÷ calls; the live page was corrected around a roughly 44% French Atelier contacted-rate standard and the observation that Longevity's high-volume days collapsed when calls-per-lead fell.
