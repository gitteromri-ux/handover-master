## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The package contains 8 onboarding emails, 2 long-form landing pages, WhatsApp utility and marketing messages, 2 Typeform quiz specs, hero imagery, full-size mockups, and a master index/browser for downloading or opening each deliverable.
- **Established email design system** — the onboarding set matches the earlier Caitlin/Tu-me-manques French Atelier emails with a typographic `French Atelier` / `by Acadomia` wordmark, navy/gold/ivory palette, Cormorant Garamond + Inter typography, and favicon coverage; no separate graphic logo was used in either the prior emails or the onboarding set.
- **CEO-audit correction pass** — the accepted package tightened hero ratios, email word counts, conversion-phrase emphasis, CTA styling, landing-page length/gradient/flourish, WhatsApp card structure, Typeform palette tokens, and mockup regeneration after the user required a CEO-level brief audit.
- **Brand-token discipline** — the final audit removed off-palette green and dark Typeform colors, leaving the locked palette and font system as the only brand tokens across emails, landing pages, Typeform, and the index.
- **LP delivery page as handoff surface** — the landing-page deliverables are exposed through `landing-pages-delivery.html` with separate LP03 and LP06 sections, per-element headlines, and individual download buttons for desktop LP mockups, mobile LP mockups, 1920×502 desktop heroes, and 758×556 mobile heroes.
- **Implementation-ready copy standard** — after CEO/internal feedback, LP03 and LP06 copy was rewritten to remove AI tells, correct native French/place references, and position the pages as ad-conversion pages with hook, promise, proof, and trial CTA.
- **Images-only download page** — `lp-images.html` exists for the four requested LP hero images only, including regenerated Colmar Petite Venise imagery and explicit size labels for the exact desktop/mobile assets.
- **Acquisition-drip expansion** — `french-atelier-acq-drip` extends the lifecycle system with 12 emails, 12 landing pages, a quiz, a promo-claim page, and 36 exact-size images, using the established onboarding email chrome and a de-AI copy pass.
- **WhatsApp RET/ACQ expansion** — `french-atelier-wa-ret-acq` adds six WhatsApp messages, six landing pages, and 12 campaign images as a parallel retention/acquisition series.
