## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The deck converts the LLA offer into eight equal Courtney script concepts, each presented with a full name, angle, act-by-act VO, Courtney imagery, LLA navy/gold branding, and enrollment-oriented CTA language.
- **LLA, not French Atelier branding** — Courtney's series uses Longevity Life Academy positioning, healthspan/accessibility language, and enrollment CTAs instead of French Atelier or Julie creative identity.
- **Long-form ad timing** — the scripts were corrected from 30–45 seconds to 60–90 seconds after the user clarified the required format.
- **Graphic CTA card system** — each film now carries three short action-verb CTA cards mapped to script moments and website sections: LEARN/MASTER/START for films 1–2 and CONTROL/MASTER/START for film 3.
- **Result placeholder guardrail** — the deck keeps a `[RESULT]` placeholder for Courtney's verified transformation number instead of inventing a proof claim.
- **Julie separation** — the July rebuild removed Julie from the Courtney scripts, used real Courtney photos throughout, and positioned Courtney's masterclass around 40-year-old nerves, presence/access/data, and clinical-premium editorial design rather than the Julie proof-point story.
- **Unified deck format** — the active repository now packages eight entries, five options plus three films, with each entry opening on a script slide.
- **Julie-deck extraction** — after Julie scripts were briefly inserted into the Courtney deck, the work reverted Julie out and rebuilt her VO scripts as a separate PR-deck-style Julie surface under `/julie/`, preserving Courtney/Jullie separation.
- **Exact title preservation** — after the eight-script rebuild, the user objected that the top-three script names had changed from names already presented; future edits should preserve the previously presented Courtney titles unless the exact replacement wording is supplied.
- **Full-script slide format** — later commits added verbatim full-script slides for “The Guide” and “The Two Lives,” threaded act headlines through those slides, and standardized scripts 09/10 to the same VO + GRAPHIC CARD + SEE structure.
- **Script 07 direction lock** — Script 07 now uses the “Direct Path to Longevity” direction, clearer path language, renamed pillar framing, LLA + protocol linkage, and a softer close.
- **Broadcast-spec editor handoff** — `gitteromri-ux/lla-s09-s10-downloads` is the companion editor-download surface for Scripts 09/10, with Courtney FRONT/SIDE PRO and ULTRA video masters, broadcast audio normalization, and streaming faststart packaging.
- **Focused review surface** — `gitteromri-ux/courtney-review` pairs the two main Courtney cuts with supporting videos and a curated 20-banner wall. Lightweight fast-start previews protect playback while download controls keep the untouched masters.
- **Review layout preserves the assets** — banners display two per row at native proportions, with unlike design families separated and no descriptive subheadlines competing with the creative. The smeared 7A Neon 1.91:1 asset was removed rather than repaired in place.
- **Browser-safe sound behavior** — the horizontal main cut is the only video that attempts sound-on autoplay; all other videos autoplay muted and expose a manual sound control because browsers may block unprompted audio.
