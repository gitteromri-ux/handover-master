## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The project packages video ads, caption copy, download buttons, approval badges, and platform-specific posting instructions into a static review surface for immediate campaign execution.
- **Separate ad-pack repo** — campaign production moved into a new HTML repo created on 2026-06-27 rather than continuing inside the contest LP repo. This keeps paid-media review/download/playbook assets separate from the entry-flow surface.
- **Approved Ad 1/Ad 2 package** — the live page carries final Ad 1 and Ad 2 MP4s in 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16 variants with working download buttons and audio verification; Ad 1 is the Facebook feed-video choice and Ad 2 is the Instagram Reel choice.
- **Ad 3 hold state** — Ad 3 banners and reels were added as a separate fold but marked “Do not upload” because their baked-in “3 winners” language conflicts with the final neutral prize-count direction.
- **Tonight’s Posting Playbook** — the `#tonight` section gives the exact Instagram Reel and Facebook Page post workflow, including video download buttons, copy buttons, bio/CTA settings, and platform toggles.
- **Invite-friends mechanic** — the final posting copy bases entry on inviting 10+ friends to follow/like the page, keeps the prize count vague, and asks entrants to provide invite screenshots through the submission flow as proof.
- **Platform-correct CTA routing** — Instagram/Reel copy uses link-in-bio plus pinned first-comment instructions, while Facebook uses an inline link and “Learn More” CTA button; the page-level thumbnails and “Enter & Submit” links point to `win-paris-fa.vercel.app`.
- **Caption library** — the `/copy/` surface contains five Instagram caption/story options with downloadable `.txt` files and click-to-copy controls; the revised copy leads each option with the French Atelier brand name.
