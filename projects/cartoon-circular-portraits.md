## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The project converts user-supplied real photos into warm, painterly, semi-realistic cartoon portraits with thin black circular borders, then serves them through a public gallery with per-image download buttons.
- **All-source-photo coverage** — the final gallery covers all 28 uploaded source photos, after the user rejected an initial 16-photo subset.
- **Per-photo portrait frame** — each portrait should keep every person from its source photo inside the circle, preserve exact facial identity, and avoid cropping, beautifying, slimming, or changing age.
- **Public gallery as source of truth** — the later פלונטר board workflow treats `https://gitteromri-ux.github.io/cartoon-circular-portraits-1783953556/` as the only approved source for the 16 small edge medallions.
- **Poster-lock workflow** — when these portraits are composited into the board or family-box artwork, untouched text, logos, borders, hands, feet, bones, side panels, and non-target characters must stay locked.
- **Non-generative upscale rule** — after the composition is approved, upscale with algorithmic resampling/sharpening rather than regeneration so the cartoons and Hebrew/English text remain unchanged.
