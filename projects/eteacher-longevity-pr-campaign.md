## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The campaign is structured as a multi-wave PR sequence: the original four waves covered market entry, Julie Gibson Clark, Longevity Hub/app roadmap, and six-course expansion, while Wave 5 adds Bryan Johnson newsjacking articles to extend the campaign to 22 articles.
- **eTeacher-first story architecture** — the campaign leads with eTeacher entering the longevity market, not Julie alone. Julie is used as the human proof point after the corporate market-entry story lands.
- **Quote sign-off discipline** — Harel Tayeb and Julie quote blocks lead with verbatim published language plus one voice-matched sentence flagged for approval, keeping new longevity claims out of the deck unless the business signs them off.
- **Past-tense ranking correction** — Julie's Rejuvenation Olympics #2 claim is written in past tense after audit, because her historical #2 ranking changed after a 2024 rule change and a present-tense claim would fail reporter fact-checking.
- **Roadmap framing for unlaunched assets** — the Longevity Hub app, AI tutor, and six new course tracks are framed as upcoming/roadmap items rather than shipped LLA features.
- **Faculty and price locks** — PR copy can publicly name Natalie Blackbourne, Amy Jamieson, Courtney Donofrio, and Jordan Lattimore, and should use $289/month as the public price.
- **Harel quote originality** — CEO quotes must be net-new and avoid reusing previously published Harel Tayeb language; the deck/doc rebuild explicitly rewrote the quote set for on-voice originality.
- **No-price headline policy** — the campaign removed price from headlines even while retaining $289/month as the public pricing lock for body/offer accuracy.
- **Bryan Johnson newsjacking wave** — Wave 5 adds five Bryan Johnson headline-driven articles, extending the README to 22 articles across five waves while keeping the LLA message in the headers.
- **Original-file correction mode** — when correcting the PR deck/doc, content edits should be applied into the user's original files while preserving design, layout, and structure unless a design change is explicitly approved.
- **Julie-review approval surface** — `gitteromri-ux/lla-julie-review` is the separate share link for Julie approval, containing the Julie chapter and Bryan Johnson contrast chapter without the broader launch plan, with Israel references removed and a responsive mobile/desktop viewer.
- **Outreach status discipline** — the live PR tracker should distinguish inbox delivery, portal-required outlets, bounces/delays, and confirmed publication; by 2026-07-14 the campaign had 12 free PR targets plus 14 direct journalist pitches out, IssueWire required manual portal submission, and no live coverage URL was confirmed.
- **Public PR-submission kit** — `gitteromri-ux/lla-pr-kit` is the GitHub Pages execution surface for the campaign, combining free-outlet submission cards, copy buttons, full/short article bodies, monitoring notes, and a board-facing newswire tab.
- **Newswire decision framing** — the newswire board must show cost, article length, channels, timing, approval path, flagship-vs-affiliate status, online-only outlet names, and a highlighted recommended tier; vague outlet counts are not enough for CEO decisions.
- **Free-outlet execution stack** — the kit names IssueWire, MarketPressRelease, PRLog, PRUrgent, free-press-release, openPR, and PRFree as the free-outlet stack and adds 22 outlet-ready copy blocks for portal submission.
- **Public-contact correction** — the 2026-07-16 PR-kit revision removed Israel/Hebrew University references, made Harel Tayeb the public-facing media contact, and kept Omri as back-office Marketing rather than the public release contact.
- **Live-URL priority over mockups** — the active free-outlet session centered on getting real outlet placements live, with mockups only useful when they reflect actual outlet pages and clearly identify eTeacher Group.
- **Reply-monitor cadence** — the two-hour inbox monitor was paused after two quiet weeks because repeated checks were timing out and consuming credits; prnow.io ticket `#60150` remained with editorial pending a live confirmation.
