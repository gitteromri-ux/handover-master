## Rules & preferences for this project

The following source notes are retained in full because they contain the project’s decisions, rules, creative direction, delivery constraints, and known technical guardrails.

- The project adapts the established French Atelier email-review format to LLA's visual system, allowing reviewers to scan the whole series and open each complete email from one public surface.
- **Implementation is narrower than the operating brief** — the gallery contains 20 produced emails across five funnels, while the Delta Force brief defines the broader eight-funnel, 28-email automation plan.
- **Production pricing is the source of truth** — copy was reconciled to $79 as one masterclass payment and $279 monthly for the Blueprint course after earlier values conflicted with the production checkout.
- **Claims must exist in the offer** — Q&A, limited-seat, one-on-one, and similar unsupported promises were removed; email teaching points come from the published masterclass curriculum and protocol content.
- **Approved-visible assets only** — source files merely present in a website repository do not qualify. Heroes use supplied elements 01–03 or imagery visibly used on approved live pages, cropped without embedded marketing text.
- **French Atelier anatomy, LLA visual skin** — the series retains the established hero dimensions, body-length bands, CTA placement, header/footer anatomy, and personalization-token rules while using larger, premium LLA typography and branding.
- **Only approved automation names are visible** — gallery cards use Sandra's three approved funnel names and numbered email labels; invented `LGV_` labels were removed from customer-facing headings.
- **ActiveCampaign-native personalization** — implementation uses standard ActiveCampaign tags such as `%FIRSTNAME%`, `%LGVMASTERCLASSSESSIONDATE%`, `%LGVMASTERCLASSZOOMLINK%`, and account-management links rather than unresolved `{{token}}` placeholders.
